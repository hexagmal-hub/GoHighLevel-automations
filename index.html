What Are GoHighLevel Automations

GoHighLevel automations are automated workflows that perform actions based on specific triggers. When a lead fills out a form, books an appointment, or sends a message, the system automatically responds. These actions can include sending messages, updating lead status, assigning tasks, or moving leads through sales pipelines.
